Agro Models

Integrating Environmental, Hydrological, and Process System Modeling for Smarter Agriculture and Sustainable Water Treatment

Agro Models (Private) Limited is dedicated to advancing sustainable development through the power of modeling and simulation. We develop mechanistic, hybrid and data-driven models that bridge agriculture, water treatment, and environmental systems into one integrated framework.

Our work begins with smart and precision agriculture, optimizing nutrient management, irrigation efficiency, and crop performance. Beyond the field, we extend this modeling approach to water and wastewater treatment processes, ensuring that treated water meets environmental standards while maintaining cost and operational efficiency.

What distinguishes Agro Models (Private) Limited is its systems-level perspective. Our models do not stop at plant roots or treatment plant outlets; they trace how human interventions ripple across rivers, aquifers, and soils, linking agricultural productivity with water security and environmental health. By coupling these domains, we aim to create a comprehensive, physically based modeling framework that can also integrate data analytics and AI where appropriate, enhancing prediction, adaptability, and decision-making for clients across sectors.
