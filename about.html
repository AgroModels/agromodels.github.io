Who we are

Agro Models (Private) Limited was founded with a singular purpose — to improve the quality of life in Sri Lanka by advancing how we understand, manage, and protect our natural resources. Our focus begins with water — the most vital and yet most vulnerable resource — and extends across agriculture and environmental systems that depend on it.

The company was born from the urgent need to reduce the toxic substances entering Sri Lanka’s water bodies and agricultural lands, and to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of water treatment processes across the country. By doing so, we aim not only to safeguard public health and the environment but also to create a foundation for evidence-based, data-driven policymaking that leads to lasting national impact.

But Agro Models was never meant to serve only Sri Lanka. Our vision is global. We recognize that Sri Lanka is home to extraordinary mathematical and engineering talent — talent that can contribute meaningfully to the world’s most complex environmental challenges. Through Agro Models, we harness this capability to develop advanced, process-based, and hybrid digital models for agriculture and water systems — models that can benefit industries, governments, and research institutions around the world.

At its heart, Agro Models is about integration: of science and society, of mathematics and environmental care, of local expertise and global application. Our mission is to build models that don’t just simulate systems — but transform how they are understood, managed, and sustained.
